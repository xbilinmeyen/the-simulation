# THE SIMULATION
### Role-Play Simulation · v1.0

[English](README.md) · [Türkçe](README.tr.md)

**Who would you be if you lived another life?**

THE SIMULATION is an open-ended, fictional life simulation you can play in your own AI chat. You step into a character's life, with their history, relationships, resources, and responsibilities. You decide what you do and say. The world responds according to its own conditions.

Everyone here has a life of their own. People work, make plans, change their minds, and act in their own interests. A persuasive conversation does not open every door. An attempt may succeed, remain unfinished, or fail. The world is not arranged to lead you toward success, nor is it made harder to punish you.

## What is RPS?

**RPS stands for Role-Play Simulation.** It is the name used in this project for an approach that combines playing a character with simulating an independent world. It is a project term, not a claim to have invented a new or universally recognized genre.

You control your character's intentions, words, and attempted actions. The AI manages the environment, other people, and consequences based on established conditions. Declaring an outcome does not automatically make it happen: “I convince them” is an attempt, and the other person still has their own knowledge, interests, and boundaries.

Time, money, possessions, abilities, relationships, and commitments matter. Buying something, owning it, carrying it, and using it are different states. An offer is not completed work or money already received. A character's success depends on what the world actually supports, rather than their place at the center of the conversation.

RPS leaves room for ordinary life, uncertainty, success, and failure. There is no mandatory crisis or predetermined ending. The simulation does not choose your feelings, decisions, or next move for you.

## How you play

After settings, five short preference questions begin directly, one at a time. Write “skip” to pass the current question, or “skip all” to pass the remaining interview. Then choose **1 · Goal-Directed World**, **2 · Fully Open World**, **3 · Imagined World**, or **4 · Tutorial**. The first two offer three different lives; the third builds on your own world idea. The tutorial is a separate trial and returns to the choice screen afterward. Character preferences expressed in existing answers are retained.

In **WORLD**, write your actions and dialogue. Use **INNER VOICE** to ask about your character's knowledge, memories, or physical condition. Fictional time does not pass during inner voice, and other people cannot hear your thoughts.

Startup settings let you independently enable or disable **bodily needs, detailed inventory tracking, and image generation**. With images on and suitable tools available, pixel-art images accompany the opening and major scenes; without tools, play continues in text. Start in your preferred language and change it during play. Language support and narration quality depend on the capabilities of your AI model.

## Getting started

1. Download the **THE SIMULATION v1.0 TXT** file in your preferred source language.
2. Upload it to your AI chat.
3. Write **“Start THE SIMULATION using the rules in this file.”**
4. Read the opening notice and indicate whether you accept it, then choose your life.

The TXT version requires no separate application, server, or external API key. A skill package is also available for environments supporting skills.

Write **“Pause”** to take a break, **“Resume”** to continue, or **“Save”** to get a record you can carry to another chat. Upload both the rules and your save in the new chat.

## What you should know

THE SIMULATION is fiction run by an AI following written rules. It does not reproduce real life exactly or provide professional advice. Consistency, memory, translation, and adherence to rules vary by model, and errors can occur. The file alone provides no persistent memory, verified randomness, or world running in the background.

The project files contain no independent system that automatically sends your conversations to the developer. Your AI platform's own data and privacy policies apply.
